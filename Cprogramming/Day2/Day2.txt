Problem: Squirrel Play
Problem Statement

The squirrels in a park spend most of the day playing.

The squirrels play based on the following conditions:

If the temperature is between 60 and 90 (inclusive), the squirrels will play.
If it is summer, the upper temperature limit becomes 100 instead of 90.

Write a C program that reads the temperature and whether it is summer, then prints whether the squirrels will play.

Input
An integer temperature
An integer isSummer
1 → Summer
0 → Not Summer
Output

Print:

Squirrels Play if the squirrels will play.
Squirrels Do Not Play otherwise.
Sample Input 1
Enter Temperature: 70
Is it Summer? (1-Yes, 0-No): 0
Sample Output 1
Squirrels Play
Sample Input 2
Enter Temperature: 95
Is it Summer? (1-Yes, 0-No): 0
Sample Output 2
Squirrels Do Not Play
Sample Input 3
Enter Temperature: 95
Is it Summer? (1-Yes, 0-No): 1
Sample Output 3
Squirrels Play
